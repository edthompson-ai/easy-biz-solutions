# Easy Biz Solutions

**Websites, online booking, and ordering for local Austin businesses.**

<sub>[← Ed Thompson's profile](https://github.com/edthompson-ai)</sub>

---

## Result

Launched Oct 26, 2026. Redesign shipped Nov 1, 2026 through a preview link with a logged rollback, live at [easybizsolutions.com](https://easybizsolutions.com).

## Problem

Small Austin shops need a simple site that brings in calls and bookings, without paying an agency.

## What I did

Mapped the business into three service lines: restaurant ordering, website builds and redos, and party rental booking. Then directed a team of AI agents through build, review, and deploy.

**Wrote a clear launch offer** with an on-time guarantee and fixed change terms, in English and Spanish.

**Set a design bar** for a gallery of sample sites by business type. Each sample is marked as a fictional business.

**Shipped a full redesign** to a preview link first, then live, with a logged rollback point.

## What broke and what I changed

**The booking preview failed its first review.** On Nov 1, 2026, review found sideways scrolling and a cut-off button on a 320 px phone, and a "You're booked" message shown before times were locked. All were fixed, the page now says "Request received," and the recheck passed the same day.

**A phone screenshot was too small to judge.** On Oct 23, 2026, review refused to grade a tiny full-page shot and asked for section-by-section phone crops. The recheck on those crops passed, and section crops are now required for phone review.

## Decisions

**Promise the delivery date, not sales.** The guarantee covers delivering the page on the agreed date. Results depend on the shop, so the business makes no sales promise.

**Fictional sample sites, clearly labeled.** Shops see real designs without anyone copying a real business or implying clients that don't exist.

**"Request received," not "You're booked."** Double bookings weren't blocked yet, so the page only promises what is true.
